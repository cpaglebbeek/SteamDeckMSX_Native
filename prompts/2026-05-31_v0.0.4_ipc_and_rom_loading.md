4. **QML uitbreiding** — `Main.qml` met FileDialog (QtQuick.Dialogs), state-LED + state-label, Y-shortcut = stop, toast-feedback. `AddRomCard.qml` apart gestyled (accent-warm border). `Toast.qml` info/warning/error 3s-fade overlay. `CartridgeBrowser.qml` Loader delegate met conditional component.
5. **Tests** — `tests/test_msxcore.cc` 6 cases (initial state, path roundtrip, probe-no-path Failed, probe-true Probed, stop-idle no-op, sendCommand-idle warn). 6/6 ✅.
6. **Docs** — `docs/openmsx_control_protocol.md` als interpretatie-spec (XML-stream-formaat, command-set v0.0.4, v0.0.5+ planning, Mac-quirks).
